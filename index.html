FAQ

Asked often

Can I pay online by card?

No. There is no card payment on this site at all. Pay the delivery man in cash, or transfer to our bank account after you order.

How long does grated coconut keep?

Three days in the fridge, sealed. Freeze the pack unopened on the day it arrives and it keeps for a month.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Yes. We call before every run — change the quantity, the day or the address on that call, or cancel outright at no cost.

Where do you deliver?

Colombo and suburbs on the Wednesday and Saturday runs. The rest of the island goes by courier and takes one day longer.

What is in the pack?

Grated coconut. Nothing else — no preservative, no sugar, no colour, no anti-caking agent.
